03 / A FEW POSSIBLE STARTING POINTS

Small ideas worth
exploring.

Not assumptions about what you need—just examples we could discuss.

IDEA 01

Help with a current matter

Take on a defined research question, prepare a diligence checklist or help keep documents and next steps organised for a transaction.

IDEA 02

Enhance routine workflow automations

Identify opportunities to automate recurring workflows, including HR administration, shared inbox triage, document intake and matter tracking. I could help map existing processes, test practical automations and build in appropriate human review and confidentiality safeguards.

IDEA 03

Track a policy topic

Prepare a short, source-linked update on a U.S. or EU critical minerals policy development, relevant funding announcement or agency initiative.

IDEA 04

Support follow-up on opportunities

Help keep an organised list of possible contacts and partnerships, do background research and draft follow-ups for approval.

IDEA 05

Lobbying and government relations support

Draw on my existing network and experience working with congressional offices and policy stakeholders to support lobbying and government engagement efforts. I could help identify relevant decision-makers, research policy priorities, prepare briefing materials and facilitate appropriate introductions, subject to applicable lobbying requirements.

IDEA 06

Business development — GDPR, privacy and technology

Support business development around Serus Legal’s work on intellectual property, product development, GDPR and privacy compliance, and data protection for technology, e-commerce and service businesses. I could research prospective clients, develop targeted outreach and help prepare practical thought leadership to strengthen the firm’s presence and position it as a leading voice in these areas.
