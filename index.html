04 / PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION

A 90-day pilot.
Evidence before expansion.

Start small, validate with the teams and measure real operating impact before proposing a permanent role or larger build.

01 / DAYS 1–30

Discover & prioritize

  • Interview team stakeholders
  • Map client and marketplace journeys
  • Audit current workflow tools
  • Select the first high-value use case
Deliverable: opportunity map
02 / DAYS 31–60

Prototype & activate

  • Test a qualified opportunity tracker
  • Develop funding research workflow
  • Trial structured project intake
  • Launch targeted partnership outreach
Deliverable: pilots + outreach
03 / DAYS 61–90

Measure & recommend

  • Review lead quality and conversations
  • Assess workstream time savings
  • Identify commercially useful refinements
  • Present the next-stage business case
Deliverable: impact review
Suggested measures: qualified prospects, meetings booked, matters or advisory inquiries appropriately initiated, time spent on repeatable tasks and demonstrated workflow quality. No revenue claims are assumed in advance.
